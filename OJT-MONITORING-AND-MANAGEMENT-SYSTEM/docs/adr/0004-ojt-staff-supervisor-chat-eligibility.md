# Keep department-linked OJT Coordinator–Supervisor chat eligibility

**Status:** Updated by [ADR 0006](0006-explicit-ojt-account-roles-and-review-ownership.md)

Chat is available only to active OJT Coordinators and Supervisors when the Supervisor has an active placement linked to a student in that Coordinator's granted department. Students and Super Admins do not use this chat. A Supervisor without an eligible placement cannot participate. Super Admins have no access to chat content.
