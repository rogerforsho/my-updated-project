# Consolidate staff account categories

**Status:** Superseded by [ADR 0006](0006-explicit-ojt-account-roles-and-review-ownership.md)

To reduce the number of system role categories, expose only Student, Supervisor, and OJT Staff. OJT Staff is an umbrella account category; permissions continue to distinguish academic workflows, department reporting, and technical administration, with ordinary staff limited to assigned departments and overall summaries reserved for designated staff. This reduces role confusion without granting every staff member the former Coordinator, Program Head, and Admin access.
