# System Workflows

This document follows the approved role and workflow decisions in [ADR 0006](../../../docs/adr/0006-explicit-ojt-account-roles-and-review-ownership.md). The application roles are OJT Student, Supervisor, OJT Coordinator, and Super Admin. Students use the mobile app only; the other roles use web or desktop.

## 1. Student Registration and Approval

1. An eligible fourth-year ICS or IBE student registers in the mobile app with their name, school email, student number, course, and year level.
2. The system creates a pending account and sends an email-verification link. A matching email or student-number conflict is rejected without changing the existing account or linked records.
3. After email verification, an OJT Coordinator reviews the identity evidence and academic eligibility within their department grant.
4. The Coordinator approves or rejects the registration. Rejected students correct and resubmit using the same account; they do not create a replacement identity.
5. An active student signs in through the mobile app. Web and desktop routes deny student access.

## 2. Student Preparation and Placement

1. An active student submits exactly two pre-deployment gateway documents: the **Pre-OJT Orientation Certificate** and **Endorsement Letter**. Registration ID front/back images are identity-review evidence, not additional gateway documents.
2. The OJT Coordinator reviews both documents. Company placement remains locked until required documents are approved.
3. The student proposes a Practicum Schedule with dates, work days, shift times, lunch interval, and work modality. The Coordinator reviews it against the approved institutional rules and company requirements.
4. The student selects an existing Host Training Establishment (HTE) or requests an unlisted HTE. Optional Business Permit or MOA copies support verification; the application does not create or sign an MOA.
5. The OJT Coordinator verifies the HTE, approves branches, and assigns the student and an active Supervisor. The Coordinator alone controls company reassignment.
6. Remote work and off-site activity require explicit, time-limited approval. GPS is contextual review evidence; it does not automatically reject attendance. The app does not collect Wi-Fi SSIDs.

## 3. Attendance and Human Review

1. The student captures Time In and Time Out in the mobile app with a live selfie, point-in-time GPS evidence when available, and an event time from the device.
2. If offline, the app retains the event and photo in its private local queue. On sync, the backend stores a separate server receipt time. Syncing does not verify attendance or replace the event time.
3. Attendance remains pending until a Supervisor explicitly reviews the complete record and its evidence. A Time Out arriving after an earlier decision returns the record to pending review.
4. Supervisors review only their assigned students. An OJT Coordinator may adjudicate a specific exception with a reason and audit entry; no role approves attendance automatically or because of a timeout.
5. Only verified attendance earns credited hours. The approved lunch interval is excluded. Missing, rejected, or pending evidence earns no credit.

## 4. Reports, Requirements, and Incidents

1. Students submit daily accomplishment reports and any other required OJT reports through the mobile app.
2. The assigned Supervisor approves or returns daily accomplishment reports with feedback. A routine report does not pass through a second Coordinator approval queue.
3. The OJT Coordinator oversees academic requirements and department-level progress.
4. Student incident reports are private to the OJT Coordinator within the student's department scope. Supervisors, companies, and other students cannot view them.
5. Gateway documents and incident evidence are served through authorized private-storage access, not public links.

## 5. Supervisor Evaluation and Student Appeal

1. A Supervisor submits midterm and final evaluations for assigned students using the approved rubric.
2. The student can view their own evaluation and submit an appeal with a reason and optional supporting details.
3. The OJT Coordinator decides the appeal within their department scope. The original Supervisor evaluation is preserved; an adjusted outcome is stored as a separate appeal decision.
4. The OJT Coordinator records the student's final OJT grade on the institutional 1.00–5.00 scale after required academic conditions are satisfied. Grades 1.00–3.00 pass and finalize OJT with a certificate; grades above 3.00 remain recorded without finalization or a certificate.

## 6. Coordinator-Supervisor Communication

1. Active OJT Coordinators and Supervisors use the web-only private pair conversation when they jointly support an active placement in the Coordinator's department.
2. The conversation retains read-only history when the pair has no qualifying active placement; sending resumes if eligibility returns.
3. Messages are permanent plain text, no more than 4,000 characters. Students and Super Admins cannot use chat or read its content.
4. Each message creates a generic notification that links to the conversation without revealing message text.

## 7. Super Admin and System Administration

1. Super Admins use individual accounts with MFA. Two designated Super Admin accounts enroll MFA independently and may help one another recover access; provider recovery is the fallback if both are locked out.
2. Super Admins manage account access and system configuration, and review security audit metadata. They do not routinely access academic records, attendance, student files, incident reports, or chat content.
3. Deactivation blocks future access while preserving OJT history. Hard deletion is limited to cleanup of a newly created, incomplete account when provisioning itself fails.

## 8. What-If Analysis and Finalization

1. A Coordinator may model a proposed weekly attendance pace and missed weeks to estimate a student's completion date. Offline attendance can sync at any time; its age never changes its pending review status.
2. What-if results are read-only and never change schedules, assignments, attendance, grades, or student status.
3. When the Coordinator finalizes completion, attendance, reports, evaluations, and the final grade become read-only historical records. The original data and audit history remain available according to the retention policy.
