# Test & Evaluation Plan

## Evaluation Standard (ISO/IEC 25010:2023)
Evaluate functional suitability, performance efficiency, usability, and security-related features. Report that security-related features were evaluated; do not claim unconditional certification or a full security audit.

## Functional Testing

Verify approved workflows and enforce role boundaries:
- **Authentication and roles:** Student registration, email verification plus Coordinator approval, one-time setup/recovery links, role guards, and Super Admin MFA.
- **Account safety:** Email conflicts preserve existing Auth users and linked records; deactivation preserves OJT history.
- **Attendance:** Online/offline capture, device event time versus server receipt time, pending human review after sync, and credited hours only after verification.
- **Placement:** ICS/IBE-scoped Coordinator access, HTE/branch verification, Supervisor assignment, and time-limited off-site approvals.
- **Documents and reports:** Exactly two pre-deployment gateway documents; Supervisor decisions for daily accomplishment reports; Coordinator-only incident visibility and gateway review.
- **Evaluations and completion:** Both passing Supervisor evaluation stages and no pending appeal are required; a Coordinator records a constrained 1.00–5.00 final grade; grades 1.00–3.00 may finalize, while grades above 3.00 remain recorded without completion; finalized academic records and appeals stay locked, including after certificate revocation; public certificate lookup omits the grade.
- **What-if analysis:** Read-only scenario results do not change schedules, assignments, attendance, or grades.
- **Administration:** Account and system configuration actions require Super Admin MFA and do not expose academic records.

## Usability Testing

Evaluate with OJT Students, OJT Coordinators, Supervisors, and Super Admins:
- Interface clarity and responsive presentation.
- Navigation and task completion.
- Error prevention and clear feedback for loading, empty, and error states.

## Compatibility Testing

- Web: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari on desktop/laptop form factors.
- Mobile: Android and iOS Expo/React Native on camera-enabled smartphones.

## Security-Related Testing

Evaluate protection mechanisms:
- Unauthenticated access rejection through middleware and Supabase RLS.
- Role and IDOR checks for student, Supervisor, Coordinator, and Super Admin access.
- Chat RLS checks: unrelated users, Super Admins, inactive accounts, and non-eligible pairs cannot read or write messages; archived participants can read history but cannot send; read markers and notification status can be changed only by their owner.
- Chat messages cannot be updated or deleted by participants; generic chat notifications do not expose message content.
- Private storage isolation and short-lived signed URLs for selfies and submissions.
- Injection, input tampering, email collision, duplicate attendance sync, and stale/offline event review behavior.
