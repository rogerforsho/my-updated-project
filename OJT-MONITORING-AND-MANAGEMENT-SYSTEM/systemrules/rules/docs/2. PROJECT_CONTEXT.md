# Project Context

## Project
**Cross-Platform On-the-Job Training (OJT) Monitoring and Management System** for **Colegio de Montalban**

## Target Beneficiaries & Scope Boundary
Specifically designed for **fourth-year students** under:
1. **Institute of Computing Studies (ICS)** (e.g., BSIT, BSCS)
2. **Institute of Business and Entrepreneurship (IBE)** (e.g., BSBA, BSA, BSHM)

### Out-of-Scope:
- 1st to 3rd-year students
- Other institutes and programs outside ICS and IBE
- Other institutions
- Job application systems / hiring systems
- Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) legal transactions
- Payroll systems
- General Human Resource (HR) management
- Full offline operation; only student attendance actions are queued offline on mobile and verified after synchronization

## Background & Problems Solved
Replaces fragmented, manual OJT processes involving:
- Manual paper logbooks & Daily Time Records (DTR)
- Difficulty computing rendered and remaining internship hours
- Physical report and requirement submissions
- Scattered and lost physical documents
- Delayed communication and approval workflows
- Manual attendance validation difficulties
- Inconsistent or delayed record tracking

## Target Users & Roles
1. **OJT Student:** Mobile-only account for personal attendance, requirements, reports, OJT progress, and notifications.
2. **Supervisor:** Web/desktop account for assigned trainee attendance review, accomplishment report decisions, feedback, and performance evaluations.
3. **OJT Coordinator:** Web/desktop account for ICS/IBE-scoped registration, gateway documents, placements, exceptions, academic oversight, and final grades.
4. **Super Admin:** Web/desktop account for account access and system configuration, protected by MFA and excluded from routine academic records and chat content.
5. **IT Experts / Evaluators:** External evaluators who assess technical quality using selected ISO/IEC 25010:2023 criteria; not application accounts.

## Hardware & Environment Constraints
- **Minimum Development Machine:** Intel Core i5 / AMD Ryzen 5 or equivalent, **8 GB RAM**, 256 GB SSD.
- **Mobile Testing:** Camera-enabled smartphone (iOS / Android) for selfie-based attendance verification.
- **Infrastructure:** Centralized Supabase cloud backend (Database, Auth, Storage) accessible via Web and Mobile.
