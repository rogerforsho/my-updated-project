# Requirements Specification

Labels: **RV** research-validated; **UA** user-approved; **TD** technical design decision.

## Authentication & Role Management
- **FR-AUTH-001 [UA]** Fourth-year students from ICS and IBE may self-register.
- **FR-AUTH-002 [UA]** A student account becomes active only after both email verification and authorized OJT Coordinator approval.
- **FR-AUTH-003 [UA]** The OJT Coordinator approves legitimate student registrations.
- **FR-AUTH-004 [UA]** Student identity data includes email, student number, course, and year level.
- **FR-AUTH-005 [UA]** Each user has exactly one user-facing role: OJT Student, Supervisor, OJT Coordinator, or Super Admin.
- **FR-AUTH-006 [UA]** Super Admin manages technical account access and system configuration; Super Admin sessions and actions require MFA.
- **FR-AUTH-007 [TD]** Password recovery uses rate-limited, single-use email links. Student requests and password entry stay in the mobile app; web roles use the web portal.
- **FR-AUTH-008 [UA]** Students use the mobile application only; Supervisors, OJT Coordinators, and Super Admins use web or desktop access.

## Student Profile & OJT Application
- **FR-APP-001 [UA]** Students must complete student profile details before deployment.
- **FR-APP-002 [UA]** Students submit exactly two mandatory pre-deployment gateway documents: **Pre-OJT Orientation Certificate** and **Endorsement Letter**. Student ID front/back images are separate identity-review evidence.
- **FR-APP-003 [UA]** OJT Coordinator reviews and verifies both gateway documents; company assignment is locked until they are approved.
- **FR-APP-004 [UA]** Students fill out their proposed **Practicum Work Schedule** (start/end dates, weekly work days, daily shift time in/out, lunch break, and work modality).
- **FR-APP-005 [UA]** Coordinator reviews, validates, and approves the practicum schedule against institutional/CHED guidelines (max 8 hrs/day, max 40 hrs/week, no graveyard shifts).

## Companies & Assignments
- **FR-COMP-001 [UA]** OJT Coordinator manages verified HTE records and approved branches in their granted department.
- **FR-COMP-002 [UA]** A student may request a new HTE or select an existing one; the OJT Coordinator verifies the request before placement.
- **FR-COMP-003 [UA]** OJT Coordinator assigns eligible students to approved HTE branches and Supervisors.
- **FR-COMP-004 [UA]** A company may have multiple trainees.
- **FR-COMP-005 [UA]** A supervisor may supervise multiple assigned trainees.
- **FR-COMP-006 [UA]** Company reassignments or modifications are managed exclusively by the Coordinator.

## Attendance & Selfie Verification
- **FR-ATT-001 [RV/UA]** Students record attendance in the mobile app; assigned Supervisors and scoped Coordinators review it through web or desktop.
- **FR-ATT-002 [TD]** Normal attendance cycle: Time In → OJT Shift → Time Out.
- **FR-ATT-003 [TD]** One attendance session per approved OJT day.
- **FR-ATT-004 [UA]** Students cannot directly edit verified or finalized attendance records.
- **FR-ATT-005 [UA]** Weekend attendance is rejected by default unless specifically approved in the verified schedule.
- **FR-ATT-006 [UA]** System determines late status based on verified practicum schedule cutoff time plus grace period.
- **FR-ATT-007 [TD]** Late status requires an assigned schedule; a total-hour goal alone cannot determine lateness.
- **FR-ATT-008 [UA]** The mobile app may queue attendance while offline. On sync, the event remains pending; device-captured event time and server receipt time are stored separately, and only an explicit human decision can verify it.
- **FR-ATT-010 [UA]** Offline attendance has no maximum sync delay; delayed events remain unverified until a Supervisor explicitly reviews them.
- **FR-ATT-009 [UA]** Time Out received after an earlier attendance decision returns the record to pending review; attendance is not credited until the completed record is verified.
- **FR-GPS-001 [TD]** Mobile attendance capture records point-in-time device GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, and reported accuracy) and the device-captured event time exclusively during Time In and Time Out actions without continuous background tracking. The backend records the receipt time when it receives the event.
- **FR-GPS-002 [UA]** Point-in-time GPS coordinates serve as contextual location metadata for supervisor review, avoiding automated boundary lockouts.
- **FR-GPS-003 [UA]** Students performing off-site errands, client assignments, or remote tasks can provide contextual activity notes alongside their GPS geotag.
- **FR-SELFIE-001 [UA]** Selfie capture enforces live front-camera input with an oval viewfinder guide; gallery/photo picker upload is disallowed.
- **FR-SELFIE-002 [TD]** On-device face presence validation verifies a human face is present before capture is enabled (mitigates random/black photos; biometric identity matching is excluded).
- **FR-SELFIE-003 [TD]** Captured selfie is embedded with an anti-spoofing watermark HUD (timestamp, GPS coordinates, student name, and student ID).
- **FR-SELFIE-004 [UA]** Supervisor review dashboard provides Smart Compliance Triage (Face Presence flag, GPS In-Bounds flag, On-Time status) with 1-click batch verification for clean logs.
- **FR-SELFIE-005 [UA]** Unverified or rejected attendance is not credited toward rendered hours.

## Progress & Hour Computation
- **FR-PROG-001 [RV]** Automatically compute rendered and remaining internship hours in real-time.
- **FR-PROG-002 [RV]** Display rendered hours, remaining hours, and progress percentage to students and coordinators.
- **FR-PROG-003 [TD]** Credited hours derive exclusively from verified, approved attendance records.
- **FR-PROG-004 [TD]** Required hours are configurable per program/assignment (e.g., ICS vs IBE hours).
- **FR-PROG-005 [UA]** Finalized internship records become read-only upon completion.
- **FR-PROG-006 [TD]** Coordinator completion requires both a passing midterm and final Supervisor evaluation with no pending appeal; the Coordinator records a 1.00–5.00 final grade. Grades 1.00–3.00 pass and may finalize OJT; grades above 3.00 are recorded but do not issue a certificate or finalize OJT. Finalization locks attendance, reports, evaluations, appeals, schedules, assignments, progress, and the grade. Public certificate verification does not expose the grade.

## Digital Reports & Requirements Submission
- **FR-REP-001 [RV]** Students submit weekly/monthly OJT accomplishment reports digitally.
- **FR-REP-002 [RV]** Real-time submission status visibility (submitted, reviewed, approved, rejected) with remarks.
- **FR-REP-003 [TD]** Uploaded files are validated for size and approved file formats (e.g., PDF, PNG, JPG).
- **FR-REP-004 [UA]** Supervisors review assigned students' daily accomplishment reports and approve or return them with feedback. Coordinators oversee academic exceptions without duplicating routine Supervisor approvals.
- **FR-INC-001 [UA]** Student incident reports are visible to the OJT Coordinator within the student's department scope and hidden from Supervisors, companies, and other students.

## Performance Evaluation
- **FR-EVAL-001 [UA]** Company Supervisors evaluate assigned students' performance.
- **FR-EVAL-002 [TD]** Evaluation criteria adhere to the approved institutional evaluation form and scoring rubrics.
- **FR-EVAL-003 [UA]** Coordinators monitor submitted evaluations, decide student appeals while preserving the original evaluation, and record the final OJT grade on the institutional 1.00–5.00 scale.

## Notifications & Announcements
- **FR-NOTIF-001 [RV]** Centralized notification system for status updates, approval alerts, and review feedback.
- **FR-ANNC-001 [UA]** Super Admin and OJT Coordinators can create and broadcast announcements within their authorized scope.

## Coordinator–Supervisor Chat
- **FR-CHAT-001 [UA]** Only active OJT Coordinators and Supervisors may chat; a pair is eligible when the Coordinator's department matches the course department of at least one active placement assigned to that Supervisor. Students and Super Admins have no chat access.
- **FR-CHAT-002 [UA]** Each eligible Coordinator–Supervisor pair has one conversation, created when either participant sends its first message.
- **FR-CHAT-003 [UA]** Chat is online-only and web-only in v1. Messages are plain text, non-empty, and limited to 4,000 characters.
- **FR-CHAT-004 [UA]** New messages are delivered live to participants in the open conversation.
- **FR-CHAT-005 [UA]** Participants see unread counts, and opening a conversation marks its incoming messages read without sender-facing seen receipts.
- **FR-CHAT-006 [UA]** When a pair has no active matching placement, its conversation remains available as read-only history; sending resumes if a qualifying active placement returns.
- **FR-CHAT-007 [UA]** Each new message creates a generic system notification linked to its conversation, without the message text. Coordinators and Supervisors can access a shared notification inbox and bell badge.
- **FR-CHAT-008 [UA]** Existing account-deletion cascades remove associated chat history and related notifications.

## Monitoring & Administrative Reports
- **FR-MON-001 [UA]** OJT Coordinator accesses department-level monitoring summaries for granted ICS / IBE departments.
- **FR-MON-002 [TD]** All metrics and summaries are computed from live operational data.
- **FR-ADMIN-001 [UA]** Super Admin manages account access and system configuration without routine academic record or chat-content access.
- **FR-ADMIN-002 [TD]** Super Admin privileges remain distinct from academic OJT approval authority and require MFA.
- **FR-SCENARIO-001 [UA]** What-if analysis is read-only and cannot change placements, schedules, attendance, or grades.

## Non-Functional & Evaluation Standards (ISO/IEC 25010:2023)
- **Functional Suitability:** All 12 core functional areas operate as specified in the capstone manuscript.
- **Performance Efficiency:** Fast load times, indexed queries, and minimal bandwidth consumption on 8 GB RAM dev environments.
- **Usability:** Clean, intuitive UI/UX with responsive web access and native mobile controls.
- **Security:** Strict Row-Level Security (RLS), signed storage URLs, secure password handling. *(Reported as "security-related features were evaluated".)*
