# Colegio de Montalban OJT Monitoring

This system coordinates the practicum activities of eligible fourth-year ICS and IBE students and the staff who support their placements.

## Language

**Practicum Schedule**:
A planned work arrangement for a student's OJT placement, including work days, shift times, lunch interval, work modality, and date range.
_Avoid_: Shift plan, work schedule (when referring specifically to the student proposal)

**Attendance Record**:
A student's daily record of time in, time out, and the evidence used to review attendance.
_Avoid_: Timesheet (unless referring to an external document)

**Attendance Exception**:
An attendance record that needs human review to resolve a flagged issue or correction. The Supervisor reviews assigned students' records; an OJT Coordinator may adjudicate an exception when the Supervisor cannot.
_Avoid_: Pending attendance (a pending record may not need staff intervention)

**Credited Hours**:
Verified attendance time counted toward a student's required practicum hours, excluding only the portion that overlaps the approved schedule's lunch interval.
_Avoid_: Raw hours, elapsed hours (when referring to the amount counted toward completion)

**Active Account**:
A system account enabled to use the workflows allowed by its assigned role.
_Avoid_: Approved user (approval may refer to an individual request or record)

**OJT Coordinator**:
A school account responsible for academic OJT workflows within its granted department scope.
_Avoid_: OJT Staff, Program Head

**Super Admin**:
A system account responsible for account provisioning, system settings, and backup and audit metadata.
_Avoid_: Admin, System Administrator

**OJT Student**:
A fourth-year ICS or IBE student whose OJT activity is managed by the system.
_Avoid_: Trainee (when referring to the account role)

**HTE Branch**:
A specific approved work location belonging to a Host Training Establishment.
_Avoid_: Geofence (a geofence is location evidence configured for a branch)

**Supervisor Onboarding Invitation**:
A request for a company Supervisor to acknowledge their system account before students are assigned to them.
_Avoid_: MOA (an onboarding invitation is not a mutual agreement)

**HTE Verification Request**:
A student's request for an OJT Coordinator to verify and add an unlisted Host Training Establishment and its proposed branch.
_Avoid_: Company request

**Student Account Registration**:
A student's request to create a system account using identity and academic eligibility details.
_Avoid_: OJT application (when referring to account creation)

**Account Review**:
A review of a student's registration that determines whether their system account may become active.
_Avoid_: OJT requirements review

**Pre-deployment Requirements**:
The four documents an active student needs approved before proposing a schedule or being placed: Parent/Guardian Consent Waiver (notarized; photo or PDF), Medical Certificate, Pre-OJT Orientation Certificate and Endorsement Letter. Students already placed with an active assignment are exempt from documents added later.
_Avoid_: Student account registration (when referring to pre-deployment submissions); Gateway credentials; MOA (out of scope)

**Required Hours**:
The practicum hours a student must complete, set by program: BSIT 486, BS-CPE 240, BSBA-HRM 300, BSENTREP 300. A student's own `required_hours` wins, otherwise the program's value applies.
_Avoid_: Target hours, 486-hour track (only BSIT is 486)

**Practicum Window**:
The start-to-end date range of a schedule or assignment. It needs an end date, lasts at most 5 calendar months, and must leave room to reach the Required Hours.
_Avoid_: Open-ended practicum

**Pre-deployment Review**:
A review of a student's submitted pre-deployment requirements to determine whether they are ready for placement.
_Avoid_: Account review

**Eligible OJT Coordinator–Supervisor Pair**:
An active OJT Coordinator and an active Supervisor who jointly support at least one active student placement within the Coordinator's department scope.
_Avoid_: OJT Staff–Supervisor pair

**Conversation**:
The single ongoing private message thread shared by one eligible OJT Coordinator–Supervisor pair. It remains available as read-only history while the pair has no active matching placement.
_Avoid_: Trainee-specific chat
